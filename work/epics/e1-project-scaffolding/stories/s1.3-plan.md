## What the retrospective records that a plan would have surfaced earlier

Two findings, both from `s1.3-retrospective.md`:

- **The `client_unavailable` fixture could not be built the way the design specified.** The
  design showed raising `RuntimeError` inside the dependency override, but FastAPI catches
  exceptions during dependency resolution and returns 500, bypassing the endpoint's own
  try/except. The working approach is a bad DB URL that fails at `.execute()` time rather
  than at session creation.
- **`app/core/` vs the design's `app/config.py` + `app/infrastructure/`.** Pre-existing
  skeleton code disagreed with the design spec and had to be restructured mid-story.

Both are exactly the class of thing a plan's per-task verification step exists to hit before
implementation rather than during it.

## Note on the gap itself

s1.3 is the **only** story in E1 without a plan; s1.1 and s1.2 both have one. The likeliest
explanation is that s1.3 ran in parallel with s1.2 during the project's first week, before the
lifecycle had settled. The consequence was mild — both findings above were caught and fixed
within the story — but it is recorded rather than smoothed over.
