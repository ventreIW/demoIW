**Note on the capable meta tag.** The story AC named `apple-mobile-web-app-capable`. Next 15
renders `appleWebApp.capable: true` as the standardized `mobile-web-app-capable`, which is what
current iOS honors — the Apple-prefixed name is deprecated. The capability is declared; the tag
name differs from the AC's wording. Recorded rather than glossed.
